### CC-CEDICT — what we changed

CC BY-SA requires more than a credit: it requires saying what was adapted, and
licensing the adaptation the same way.

We adapt CC-CEDICT rather than serving it verbatim. Specifically: pinyin
readings are corrected where the raw data gave a rare reading instead of the
everyday one, `u:` is normalised to `ü`, tone sandhi is applied, definitions are
trimmed to what a learner needs, and entries are ordered into per-level study
sets.

**The adapted dictionary data is available under CC BY-SA 4.0.** That obligation
attaches to the dictionary data itself. It does **not** place the Hanzi Dojo
application, its stories, or its artwork under CC BY-SA — those are not
adaptations of CC-CEDICT.

## Fonts

**The native apps redistribute font binaries; the web build does not.**

Since FAB-19 the iOS and Android builds bundle Noto Sans SC, Inter and Poppins
locally (`src/assets/webfonts/`, generated by `fetch-webfonts.mjs`) so that a
cold launch never contacts `fonts.googleapis.com` or `fonts.gstatic.com` — see
`docs/PRIVACY-AUDIT.md` F4. That **does** trigger the OFL's redistribution
conditions, so each family's licence ships beside the binaries as
`src/assets/webfonts/OFL-<family>.txt`, copied from that family's own entry in
`google/fonts`. The OFL permits this: it allows bundling and redistribution
provided the licence travels with the fonts and the fonts are not sold on their
own. None is renamed, and no Reserved Font Name is used for a modified version —
the files are byte-identical to what Google Fonts serves.

The **web** build still loads all of them from the Google Fonts CDN
(`index.html`), and Noto Sans JP for the paused Japanese track is CDN-only on
both surfaces (`src/fontLoader.js`, which returns null inside the native shell).
Mona Sans has been bundled all along (`src/assets/fonts/`).

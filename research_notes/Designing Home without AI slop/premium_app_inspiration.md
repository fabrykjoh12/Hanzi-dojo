# Premium / calm app inspiration for a "Today" home with a hero card over an illustrated background

Research date: 2026-11-12. Network note: in this sandbox, apple.com, 9to5mac.com, macstories.net, sketch.com, itsnicethat.com, opal.so and blog.duolingo.com could not be fetched (host not allowed). developer.apple.com could be fetched, and it is the primary source for the Apple Design Award (ADA) material below. Everything else comes from search-result summaries, which are weaker evidence and are marked that way.

## 1. How top calm/premium apps handle the primary daily action, imagery, type, colour restraint, dark mode and progress

### Takeaway
The sources agree on one recurring pattern: one clear next action, an interface that "gets out of the way" of the content, a restrained or curated palette (sometimes tied to time of day or conditions), and progress framed as encouragement rather than comparison. Atmosphere comes from a background that carries meaning (sky colour, weather, time), not from decoration.

### Cited Findings
**Apple Design Awards 2026 (primary source: developer.apple.com)**
- Tide Guide won Visuals and Graphics (and was also an Interaction finalist). Apple: "The app's palette is even designed to match the color of the sky throughout the day". It "integrates the new design and Liquid Glass, adding extra polish to data presentation and custom animations", and its "rich full-screen charts present a boatload of weather data... in a crisp, clear manner". — [Apple Design Awards](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/)
- Moonlitt: Moon Phase Tracker won Interaction: "a simple elegance", with "best-in-class Liquid Glass integration, and easy onboarding, it's an intuitive and beautiful way to keep your eyes on the skies." — [Apple Design Awards](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/)
- Primary: News in Depth won Social Impact (visionOS): "with a properly minimal UI, Primary wisely gets out of the way of the news, keeping the focus on the story", and does this "without once resorting to sensationalism or clickbait." — [Apple Design Awards](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/)
- grug won Delight and Fun: "the scribbled design that really pops... a little masterpiece of clever simplicity", including a "hand-drawn status bar", with "no login, no cloud syncing, nothing extraneous". — [Apple Design Awards](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/)
- Structured (a daily planner) was a 2026 Inclusivity finalist. The Outsiders: Athlete Tracker, from Gentler Stories (the makers of Gentler Streak), was an Interaction finalist, and Apple cites its "Training Readiness Score, a beautiful data visualization". — [Apple Design Awards](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/)

**Apple Design Awards 2025 (primary source)**
- Vocabulary – Learn words daily (a language and word-learning app) was a Visuals and Graphics finalist: "charming, consistent illustrations that create a clean visual style and rhythm"; "Typography and iconography feel modern and balanced". — [ADA 2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/)
- Lumy (a sun and golden-hour app) was a Delight and Fun finalist: "a simple design, carefully curated color palette, and elegant widgets". — [ADA 2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/)
- Opal was a Social Impact finalist: "a more intentional relationship with technology"; "a charming way to create focus". — [ADA 2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/)
- iA Writer was an Interaction finalist for "distraction-free writing" that helps people "tune out interruptions and focus on the words". Mela's cooking mode is "subtly dimmed and highlighted". — [ADA 2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/)
- Speechify won Inclusivity: "The design team clearly worked to reduce cognitive load". — [ADA 2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/)
- CapWords (a word-learning app built on photographed objects) won Delight and Fun ("a fun animation"). — [ADA 2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/)

**Apple Design Awards 2024 (primary source)**
- Gentler Streak won Social Impact. It has "an optimistic and encouraging vibe", and its Monthly Summary "is less about hard comparisons and more about progress", using "a subtle and consistently pleasing design language". — [ADA 2024](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024/)
- Gentler Streak team (search summary of a Sketch interview): the goal is an app where users "feel at home the first time they open it". — [Sketch blog (summary only)](https://sketch.com/blog/gentler-streak)
- Bears Gratitude won Delight and Fun: "a warm and welcoming way to establish — and, just as crucially, maintain — a daily gratitude practice". — [ADA 2024](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024/)
- The 2024 Visuals and Graphics finalists included Sunlitt (from the Moonlitt team) and Meditate. The Interaction finalists included Arc Search. — [ADA 2024](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024/)

**Apple Weather (iOS 15 redesign, 2021, which is older)**
- The backgrounds change "based on the current conditions". Apple shipped "thousands of new animated backgrounds that depict sun position, rain, clouds, and storms". The data sits in card-style modules separated into sections. — [MacRumors guide](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-15-weather-app/); [9to5Mac hands-on](https://9to5mac.com/2021/09/24/ios-15-weather-app-hands-on/)
- Commentators saw the Dark Sky influence (Apple acquired Dark Sky in 2020) as "pretty blatant". — [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/2021/06/07/revamped-ios-15-weather-app-shows-off-a-whole-lot-of-dark-sky/)

**Headspace (2024 rebrand)**
- The 2024 brand redesign, with Italic Studio, came with a custom Aperçu cut from Colophon. The orange smiley and the breath-driven animation were kept, and the orange was inspired by monastic robes (search summary). — [It's Nice That](https://itsnicethat.com/articles/italic-studio-headspace-graphic-design-project-250424); [Design Compass](https://designcompass.org/en/2024/04/30/headspace/)
- A third-party breakdown describes the home screen as showing one recommended session chosen by time of day and recent activity. This is unverified and comes from a secondary personal guide. — [Blake Crosley design guide](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/headspace)

**Things 3 (2017 design, still current)**
- Reviewers describe an interface made deliberately "invisible" so the content leads. The Today view is widely called its most-used feature. A Cultured Code motto, "We believe simple is hard", is attributed to them secondhand. — [MacStories review](https://www.macstories.net/reviews/things-3-beauty-and-delight-in-a-task-manager/); [Blake Crosley design study](https://blakecrosley.com/blog/design-study-things)

**Opal**
- The home "gem" grows "brighter, more vibrant, and more intricate as your focus improves". Its colour encodes the state: green for focused or offline time, red for time in distracting apps. Opal's brand kit says not to "overlay text on gem sculptures", so the hero imagery stays clean and the text sits beside it (search summaries of opal.so pages). — [Opal blog](https://opal.so/blog/introducing-the-new-opal-home-screen-track-your-screen-time-and-improve-your-focus); [Opal brand kit](https://brandkit.opal.so)
- A third-party walkthrough shows the achievement gems on a black background. — [screensdesign Opal](https://screensdesign.com/apps/opal-screen-time-control/?vs=132825)

**Calm**
- Third-party teardowns show onboarding and permission screens over blue-to-purple and dark-blue gradient backgrounds. No source confirmed the current Home scene. — [screensdesign: Calm onboarding](https://screensdesign.com/articles/calm-onboarding-design/)

**Duolingo (2022 path home, which is older)**
- The home screen became a single path "you'll follow step by step", launched on 1 Nov 2022, because learners were "not sure whether they're using Duolingo in the 'correct' or 'best' way" (search summary). — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/new-duolingo-home-screen-design/)

### Inferences
- Tide Guide, Lumy, Moonlitt and Apple Weather support the idea of a background that changes with time of day, conditions or state. This is the clearest premium precedent for an atmospheric illustration behind a hero. For HanziDojo, the ink-wash mountains could shift tone (dawn, day, dusk) rather than stay static decoration. This is an inference, not a sourced recommendation.
- The phrases Apple rewards most often ("gets out of the way", "reduce cognitive load", "subtle and consistently pleasing", "less about hard comparisons and more about progress") fit HanziDojo's no-guilt rule directly. Show reviews waiting and HSK progress as observations, not pressure.
- Opal's rule against overlaying text on the hero art suggests a pattern: keep the illustration's focal area clear, and place the card where the art is quiet (sky or mist), not over the peaks.
- Duolingo's path and Headspace's single recommendation both show the "one obvious next action" home, which matches one tappable hero card.

### Gaps
- The Bear, Endel, Oak, Readwise Reader, Arc Search, Apple Fitness and Apple Journal home screens were not researched, for lack of tool budget and because the press sites were blocked. Nothing about them is claimed here.
- Calm's current Home scene and Headspace's current Today screen were not verified from primary sources.
- iOS 26 Weather and Liquid Glass specifics: the Apple HIG Materials page could not be read (no body text was returned), so the "glass for the control layer only" guidance is unverified here. Check it at developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials.
- No source gave the dark-mode treatment of any of these heroes.

## 2. Language-learning and East-Asian-themed apps praised for tasteful visual identity

### Takeaway
Reviewers praise Du Chinese, Skritter and Shirabe Jisho for clean, uncluttered interfaces that follow Apple's conventions, not for strong illustration. No source found praise for Pleco's visuals. The Vocabulary app (an ADA 2025 finalist) is the strongest sourced example of illustration and typography done well in a learning app.

### Cited Findings
- Du Chinese: reviewers call the design "absurdly good" and "clean, intuitive and very well thought out". Its core interaction syncs highlighted words with the audio, and a 2024 update redesigned the flashcards to reveal pinyin before the translation (search summaries). — [Go East Mandarin review](https://goeastmandarin.com/du-chinese-review/); [All Language Resources](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/du-chinese-review); [eChineseLearning](https://www.echineselearning.com/?p=8005316)
- Shirabe Jisho: App Store reviewers say it is "clean and intuitive" with "a really nice UI that fits into the Apple design language". One reviewer wanted a more modern look. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shirabe-jisho/id1005203380)
- Skritter: "design and interface are both done really well". Its stroke-writing grid is praised. — [All Language Resources](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/?p=17141); [Mezzoguild](https://mezzoguild.com/skritter-review)
- Vocabulary – Learn words daily: "charming, consistent illustrations that create a clean visual style and rhythm"; "Typography and iconography feel modern and balanced" (ADA 2025 Visuals and Graphics finalist). — [ADA 2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/)

### Inferences
- In this category, "tasteful" means content first: the Chinese text and audio dominate, and the chrome follows native conventions. This matches HanziDojo's design rule that the Chinese should outweigh the UI chrome.

### Gaps
- No sourced design commentary was found for Pleco, HelloChinese or Kanji Study, and none for any "Hanzi" app with ink-wash art. No source showed an East-Asian app using a landscape-illustration home.

## 3. Where to find real screen references, and what to search for

### Takeaway
Use developer.apple.com/design/awards (it has the year archives, and it was reachable here) plus the screen libraries Mobbin, screensdesign.com, Lazyweb and Banani. Search by app name plus the "Home" or "Today" flow.

### Cited Findings
- The ADA pages for each year list winners and finalists with Apple's design rationale: [2026](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/), [2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/), [2024](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024/).
- The App Store "Meet This Year's Apple Design Award Winners" story collects the 2024 winners. — [App Store story](https://apps.apple.com/us/story/id1745165692)
- Mobbin is the main real-screen library. — [mobbin.com](https://mobbin.com/)
- screensdesign.com has per-app screen pages and videos for Calm, Opal and others. Its inspiration guide cites Tolan and Luvu as homes built as "an environment with an illustrated scene rather than neutral cards". — [screensdesign guide](https://screensdesign.com/guides/app-design-inspiration/); [Calm](https://screensdesign.com/explore/apps/calm/onboarding/)
- Lazyweb lists 149 Calm screens, mostly onboarding and paywall, updated May 2026. — [Lazyweb Calm](https://app.lazyweb.com/company/calm)
- wearetenet has a gallery of home-screen examples. — [wearetenet home screens](https://www.wearetenet.com/inspirations/mobile-app-design-examples/mobile-app-homescreen-design-examples)

### Inferences
- Suggested searches: in Mobbin, "Tide Guide", "Lumy", "Sunlitt", "Moonlitt", "Gentler Streak", "Structured", "Opal", "Headspace Today", "Calm Home", "Du Chinese", "Vocabulary"; and the flow filters "Home", "Dashboard" and "Progress".

### Gaps
- Mobbin's catalogue could not be checked directly (it needs a login and was not fetched), so it is not confirmed which of these apps Mobbin covers.

## 4. Patterns for a single hero card integrated with a landscape illustration

### Takeaway
The best-sourced precedents tie the background to real state (sky colour, weather, the sun's position), keep the hero art free of overlaid text, and keep the UI minimal so the scene and the one number carry the screen.

### Cited Findings
- Tide Guide's palette tracks the sky's colour through the day. — [ADA 2026](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/)
- Apple Weather's animated backgrounds depict the sun's position and the precipitation, with information in sectioned cards on top. — [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-15-weather-app/)
- Opal's gem hero changes with the state and is never overlaid with text. — [Opal blog](https://opal.so/blog/introducing-the-new-opal-home-screen-track-your-screen-time-and-improve-your-focus); [Opal brand kit](https://brandkit.opal.so)
- Primary gets out of the way of the story. Mela dims the surroundings and highlights the task. — [ADA 2026](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/); [ADA 2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/)
- Illustrated-environment homes exist in Tolan and Luvu (per a secondary guide). — [screensdesign guide](https://screensdesign.com/guides/app-design-inspiration/)

### Inferences
- For HanziDojo: one opaque or lightly frosted card set in the quiet negative space of the ink-wash scene. The scene's tone could follow the time of day, as Tide Guide and Weather do. The card should hold one large number (reviews waiting), a calm progress line toward the next HSK level, and nothing else. This is a synthesis, not a quote.

### Gaps
- No source documented exactly how Calm or Headspace put a card over their scenes, and none covered contrast or scrim technique for text over illustration.
